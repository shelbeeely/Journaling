Guide

Make a journal, step by step

From an empty page to a PDF you can upload to Amazon KDP. Every step names the button to press and what it does. Each picture marks the exact control with a number, and the list under it says what that number does.

Open the editor demoStart at step 1

The demo uses a sample book and sample calendar. Nothing you do there is saved anywhere, so try everything. About 10 minutes for the short path: steps {{ch:open}}, {{ch:day}} and {{ch:print}}. Troubleshooting is step {{ch:trouble}}.
