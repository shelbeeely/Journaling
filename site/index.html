Journalwright Studio

Design a journal.
Print it as a book.

A calm toolkit for making physical journals and planners that fit the way you live. Arrange the pages in a visual editor, get print-ready PDFs for Amazon KDP, and keep an e-ink reader in your pocket if you want one.

What it is

Journalwright Studio turns a small set of building blocks into a whole book: a day page you arrange, weekly and monthly spreads, a calendar, a tracker, back matter. You choose what goes on the page. The studio keeps the printing rules straight.

  • Block editor

    Drag check-ins, notes, time blocks and more onto the day page. See it at real size as you go.

  • Book canvas

    Every page of the book in spreads. Zoom out to plan, zoom in to fix one page.

  • Print-ready

    KDP trims, no Type 3 fonts, page limits and an overflow check on every page.

  • E-ink companion

    Optional. An Xteink X4 shows today, takes button check-ins and stays offline.

Paper first, e-ink alongside

The paper book is the record. The X4 does the few things paper cannot: it shows today without power, and it takes a one-press check-in when you have no pen.

  • Paper holds the writing, the plan and your safety plan. Monthly books in 5.5×8.5 and 8.5×11.
  • E-ink holds today on the sleep screen and the quick counts. Firmware for the Xteink X4.
  • Offline. No accounts, no feeds, no notifications. It only talks over its own hotspot.

The book canvas and the block editor

The editor opens on the whole book and zooms in one step at a time: the book, a spread, then a single day.

  • Book

    Every page in spreads, with a legend and a jump menu. Hidden pages are shown, so nothing goes missing quietly.

  • Spread

    Two facing pages, the way they print. Pinch, scroll or tap a page to zoom in, and use Back to zoom out.

  • Day

    Drag check-ins, notes, time blocks and more onto the page. A writing-space meter warns you before a page runs out of room.

  • Dozens of block types and presets: care grid, spoons, scales, time blocks, lined notes, sketch box.
  • Start from a method with one click.
  • Fixed scan zones stay fixed, so every page can be identified.
  • No account needed to design and print. Your work saves in your browser, and you can export and import it as a file.

Build your own in three steps

  1. 1

    Make your profile

    Copy journal/content/profile.example.json and fill in your name, book title, place, time zone and the modules you want.

  2. 2

    Arrange the pages

    Open the editor, drag blocks onto the day page, check the Book view, and save your layout.

  3. 3

    Build and print

    One command writes every interior and cover PDF. Upload them to Amazon KDP as a private proof first.

cd journal && npm ci && npx playwright-core install chromium
KW_PROFILE=content/profile.example.json KW_OUT=out-sample node render.mjs month 2026-10 test.ics
KW_PROFILE=content/profile.example.json KW_OUT=out-sample node editor/build.mjs   # then open editor/dist/site/index.html

Privacy, as a promise

Journals are personal. The tools are built so that your data stays yours.

  • Calendars are never committed and never uploaded unencrypted.
  • Personal books leave the build only inside a password-protected archive.
  • This site, the editor demo and the sample cards use generic sample data only.
  • No account is needed to design and print. Accounts are coming, only for saving versions and sharing.
  • The e-ink companion sends nothing anywhere unless you start it. Today it only speaks over its own hotspot; syncing over your own Wi-Fi, when it arrives, will only ever run when you press Sync, and health logs stay off unless you turn them on.

Roadmap

What is ready, what is being built now, and what is planned. Order can change; nothing here needs an account to use for designing and printing.

Ready

Working today.

  • Block editor

    Ready

    Day-page blocks with options, presets and one-click method layouts: habits, priorities, time blocks, brain dump, energy, feelings and skills, body and sensory tracking, sketch and writing space. Every block can also be sized, and blocks that make sense on the e-ink companion say so.

  • Page grid

    Ready

    Switch a page from a flowing stack to a grid of fixed rows and columns. Blocks span several of each, with overlap and minimum-size checks that explain themselves.

  • Book canvas

    Ready

    The editor opens on the whole book, then zooms to a spread, then to a single day. Page ids, jump-to-page, hidden pages and a breadcrumb that always works.

  • Unique pages and codes

    Ready

    Every page in a book has its own id and its own scan code, and the build checks that none repeat, inside a book or across books.

  • Print-ready output

    Ready

    KDP interiors and covers, in 5.5 by 8.5 and 8.5 by 11, with an overflow check on every page, no Type 3 fonts, and rules that survive printing.

  • Book scopes and volumes

    Ready

    A book can be a month, a quarter, a season, a half-year, a year, a custom range or undated. Past the paperback page limit it splits itself into balanced volumes, each with its own cover, codes and manifest.

  • Profiles

    Ready

    One file says who and where: names, location, day start, trim and which modules are on. A generic profile builds a complete sample book.

  • Versioning and accounts

    Ready

    Optional accounts keep projects with full history: commits, branches, drafts, restore and visual comparisons. Guests keep local versions in the browser and never need an account to design or print.

  • Accessibility baseline

    Ready

    A full audit against WCAG 2.2, keyboard reordering of blocks, skip links, focus rings, reduced motion and a check in CI that fails on new problems.

  • Methods library

    Ready

    Forty-four researched guides to journaling and planning methods, with sources and what each idea could become in a book or on the e-ink companion.

  • E-ink companion

    Ready

    Check-ins with zero-based and signed scales and pick-one items, a silent focus timer, the day starting at 4 a.m., a settings page on its own hotspot, and a clear split between what lives on paper and what lives on the device.

In progress

Being built now.

  • Forks, proposals and merges

    Now

    Fork a shared project, propose changes back with visual page and block diffs, discuss them, and merge with a resolver that shows both versions when the same item changed on both sides. In review.

  • Editing only when editing

    Now

    Pages open for viewing, clean and read-only. The palette and options appear when you press Edit and go away when you press Done.

  • Library and series

    Now

    A title of your own for every book, several books per project, and series that share defaults. Navigation zooms from library to series to book to spread to page.

  • E-ink accessibility

    Now

    Large text, bolder type, a button remap for one-handed and left-handed use, adjustable refresh and sleep, and a more accessible settings page.

Planned

Designed and queued.

  • Scan options

    Planned

    The send-to strip becomes a block. The scanning border can be switched off per page. The matrix code can move, resize, switch off, change format and show a printed label. Every page still gets a unique code, across all of a person's books.

  • Spread days

    Planned

    A day can cover one page or a whole two-page spread, set per day, with per-weekday defaults. Both pages keep their own scan frame, code and header.

  • Releases and reusable pages

    Planned

    Freeze a checked version as a numbered print release with the exact PDFs and manifest kept for good. Reusable pages track their versions and where they are used, so you can review an update before adopting it.

  • Accessibility options

    Planned

    A settings panel for text size, contrast, reduced motion, dyslexia-friendly type and spacing. In print: large-print layouts, high-contrast ink, text-size scales, a dyslexia-friendly font, and a left-handed layout.

  • Languages

    Planned

    Editor and site in more languages, and printed books in them too: message catalogs, fonts per script, right-to-left layouts, calendars, and a review step before a language is marked ready.

  • Account profile and locations

    Planned

    Save places once and every new book uses them for sunrise and sunset, moon times, seasons, time zone and holidays. Your location never travels with a shared or forked project.

  • Content packs and onboarding

    Ready

    Regional resource lists, transit, holidays and seasons as data you can swap, with who checked each number and when. A setup command asks a few questions and builds a sample book; guides cover making your own journal and your own pack.

  • E-ink editor and sync

    Planned

    The companion can serve the editor on its own hotspot, or on your Wi-Fi, and sync with your account only when you start a sync on the device. Health logs stay off unless you turn them on.

  • More blocks and pages

    Planned

    Matrix and next-action blocks, commonplace and collection pages, a habit plan, a future log, a year-in-pixels page, and more one-click layouts drawn from the methods library.

  • Product site and docs

    Planned

    The methods library and guides published here in a form that is written for everyone, once the personal notes are separated out.
